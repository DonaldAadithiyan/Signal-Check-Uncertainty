## Deviations from the figures handoff (summary)

1. **Page widths:** derived from SCITEPRESS.sty and cross-checked against Example.pdf, because no LaTeX is installed.
2. **Font check:** done with a pypdf equivalent, because `pdffonts` is not installed.
3. **Figure 3:** shows the three original tasks only. The correlations for walker and cheetah at K = 1/5/20 are not in any final JSON.
4. **Figure 5:** draws the scrambled distribution as mean ± sd, because only mean and sd are stored.
5. **Figure 6:** is taller than standard (5.81 cm) so its nine rows stay legible.
6. **Figure 7:** the cartpole legend is 6 pt.
7. **Figure 8:** at k = 10 the readout is below |z| = 2 on 8 of 15 models, so the spec's "14 of 15" sentence holds only for k = 0 and E.
8. **Figure 9:** the |cos(atom, v)| values are listed here rather than labelled on the plot.
9. **Figure 1:** the scatter points come from `fig_data.json`, which the final-rule script wrote; it is not in the handoff's list of sources.
